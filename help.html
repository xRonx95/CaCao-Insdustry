Farm accounts, field records and post-harvest help

Open post-harvest process guide

Use this guide in the browser or installed web app. Keep your password and field-data passphrase private.

Your first five steps

  1. Create an account, then complete your farmer profile from My farm.
  2. Open the profile review inbox. If staff request corrections, read the feedback, update your details and resubmit. Profile approval and boundary verification are separate reviews.
  3. Register your plantation. Confirm the farmer account and farm details, then draw a boundary or start GPS mapping. Finish and review the boundary before saving.
  4. Record tree inventories and harvests against the correct plantation. Confirm dates and units: hectares (ha), square meters (m²), and kilograms (kg).
  5. Open Harvest & production, choose a plantation and date range, then use Export CSV or Print / Save PDF. Wet beans and dry beans are separate measures.

Before leaving for the field

  1. Connect, sign in, and open Sync & recovery. Prepare your records and offline pages.
  2. Unlock field data using your device’s field-data passphrase. This protects pending records on this device.
  3. Allow location access for GPS mapping. Keep the mapping screen open and the device awake. Wait for a better reading if GPS accuracy is poor.
  4. Offline saving supports plantation mapping and tree inventories. Connect to save harvests, pest reports and other activities.

“Saved on this device” does not mean the server received the record. Keep the app’s data until synchronization is confirmed.

Understand your record status

Waiting to sync
An encrypted submission remains on this device. Reconnect and unlock field data to synchronize.
Needs review
Open Sync & recovery to inspect a conflict or rejected submission. Keep the draft until it is resolved.
Synced
The server acknowledged the queued submission. Check the record’s history if you need confirmation of its contents.
Unavailable
A request failed. Reconnect and reload; the system must not treat this as zero records.

Account access and review visibility

Administrators and super administrators can access all farmer records and plantations and review farmer and field staff profiles. Administrators do not need farmer assignments. Roles, account status, invitations and field staff assignments are managed by a super administrator.

If an administrator sees an empty directory while farmers have saved records, apply pending database migrations through 014_admin_operational_access.sql, then refresh the directory or choose Refresh history. Check search filters and confirm both accounts use the same Supabase project.

Field staff access

Field personnel can access their own records and assigned farmer accounts. A super administrator grants assignments in Users & access. Compare account IDs when names are ambiguous. A manual boundary does not create a GPS walking session.

Account recovery & common problems

I cannot sign in

Check your email address and password. Use Forgot password to request a recovery link. Check spam or junk mail. An expired recovery link needs a new request. For a suspended account or missing profile, contact the staff member who registered your farm.

GPS points are skipped

Move to an open area and wait for a more accurate location. Capture at least three distinct corners around the perimeter. Review the finished shape and area before saving. GPS estimates are not legal surveys.

My records have not appeared

Check Sync & recovery and confirm that you are signed in to the same account used to create the record. A pending submission may exist only on the device where you entered it. Do not clear browser storage or uninstall the app while records are pending.

How do I ask staff for help?

Provide the page name, approximate time, plantation name and exact error message. Do not send passwords, recovery links or field-data passphrases. Staff can review the profile and boundary history within their authorized access.

Maikling gabay sa Filipino

  1. Kumpletuhin ang farmer profile at tingnan ang resulta ng pagsusuri.
  2. Irehistro ang sakahan at i-save ang hangganan nito. Suriin ang hugis at lawak bago mag-save.
  3. Itala ang mga puno at ani sa tamang sakahan. Gumamit ng tamang petsa at yunit.
  4. Kapag offline, ang pagmamapa at imbentaryo ng puno lamang ang maaaring i-save. Kumonekta para sa ibang tala.
  5. Tingnan ang Sync & recovery. Huwag burahin ang datos ng app habang may hindi pa naipapadalang tala.
