INDEPENDENT CACAO REGISTRY

How information is used

This platform supports farmer profiling, plantation mapping, tree inventories and authorized agricultural reporting for Zamboanga del Norte. It is an independent system, not an official DA, PSA or RSBSA service.

Private operational records

Farmer and worker details, contact information, supporting files and exact plantation boundaries are available only to their account owners, assigned staff and authorized super administrators. These records are not included in the public community feed.

Public community posts

When you publish a cacao post, the farmer name, caption and photo you enter are visible to everyone. Do not include private home addresses, contact details or other people's personal information without permission.

Location and device drafts

Location permission is used when you request GPS mapping. Tracking pauses if the page is hidden and stops when you leave the mapping page. Saved device drafts are encrypted with a passphrase that is not stored. Delete drafts on shared devices after use.

Access, corrections and retention

You can review and correct your permitted records after logging in. Contact your organization's designated system administrator to request access reviews, corrections, deletion or a copy of your information. Published audit records are retained to support accountability.

Before an organization launches

The operating organization must publish its responsible office and privacy contact, establish retention periods and a lawful processing basis, review its consent process, and complete a privacy and security assessment. This notice describes application behavior; it is not a legal certification.

Post-harvest processing records

Batch observations, quality assessments, facility handovers, costs and sales are private operational records available to the farmer and authorized staff. Corrections retain their original history. Community posts remain public only when intentionally published. Do not enter personal details that are unnecessary for the farm or processing record.
