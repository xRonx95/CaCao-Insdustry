How tracking works

Before storage, assigned staff must record an agreed protocol and a final quality assessment in the quality workspace.

Open process measurements
  1. Allocate wet beans from a harvest.
  2. Record fermentation and drying dates and weights.
  3. Record moisture and a quality result: pass, hold or fail.
  4. A passed batch can enter storage.
  5. Record the whole batch sale, buyer and total amount in PHP.

Each stage appends a history entry. A held or failed batch needs a repeat quality check before storage. Recorded weights cannot increase. Sale amounts do not indicate that payment was received.
