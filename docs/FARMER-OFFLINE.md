# Farmer offline mapping and inventories

1. Install the current APK. While connected, log in using the farmer account email and password. Staff accounts require a connection.
2. Open Account & community > Sync & recovery. Choose a field-data passphrase of at least 12 characters, then select Prepare field records. Create the farmer profile online before preparing the phone.
3. Turn on phone location and grant the app precise location permission. Test airplane mode before leaving.
4. In Plantation management, use My Plantation, Register Plantation, Plantation Map and Tree Inventories. Walk a boundary and save the plantation, or select a plantation and save its tree inventory. These submissions remain encrypted on this device until confirmed by the server. A new queued plantation can be selected for an inventory; sync sends the plantation before its inventory.
5. When internet returns, keep the app open and unlock field data. Uploads retry automatically under the same farmer account. If the server session has expired, log in again with that account's email and password. Pending submissions prompt for the field-data passphrase after opening the workspace, then resume automatically.
6. Check Sync & recovery for confirmed receipts. Conflicts require review and are not overwritten automatically.

Offline access requires account verification within the previous 24 hours. The field-data passphrase protects local records; it is separate from the account password and cannot be recovered. Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage with pending work. Passwords are never stored in the submission queue. Automatic synchronization runs while the app is open and unlocked, not as a background Android service.

Map tools are bundled in the APK. GPS coordinates can be captured without internet, but map background tiles, photos, community posting and other farm record features require a connection. Only farmer GPS plantation mapping and tree inventory submissions are supported offline.
