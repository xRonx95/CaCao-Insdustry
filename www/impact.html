OUR PURPOSE IN PRACTICE

Supporting the people behind cacao.

Our goal is to help farmers keep clearer records, share their work and build a more visible cacao community. These are the areas the platform is designed to support.

Where we aim to make a difference

Farmer visibility

Public cacao posts give farmers a place to show their harvests, share experiences and introduce their farms through their own words and photos.

Clearer plantation records

Farm profiles bring names, locations, varieties and tree counts together, helping farmers review the information they have recorded.

Understanding cultivated land

Boundary drawing and GPS corner tools help estimate a plantation's mapped area. These estimates support record keeping and do not replace a legal land survey.

A connected community

The home page makes farmer updates available to visitors without requiring an account, creating a shared place to follow cacao stories.

Progress starts with participation

These focus areas describe the platform's purpose. We have not yet published measured outcomes for farmer income, harvest yields or environmental change.

Every farm record and shared update helps build a clearer picture of the community's work.
