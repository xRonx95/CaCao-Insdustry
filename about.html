Better records for better post-harvest decisions.

Getting to know the platform

Who can join?

Farmers can create an account through the sign-up page. Administrators sign in using an assigned account. Everyone can browse the public cacao feed.

What information becomes public?

The farmer name, update and optional photo entered when publishing a cacao post appear on the home page. Plantation records and account emails are not part of the public feed.

How do I share my cacao?

Create an account or log in, open Share cacao from the farmer menu, add an update or photo, and publish your post.
