OUR PURPOSE IN PRACTICE

Learn what improves cacao quality.

The proposed pilot will examine post-harvest record quality, downgraded assessed weight, selling prices and logged processing costs. Income improvement has not yet been demonstrated.

Where we aim to make a difference

Process consistency

Agreed protocols and dated observations make it possible to identify missed checks and review process differences across batches.

Quality review

Staff assessments include moisture, cut-test observations and grades. Storage requires the supporting records defined by the agreed protocol.

Traceability and cooperative handling

Batch handovers record facilities, named recipients and the account entering each record. A typed name is not a digital acceptance signature.

Recorded economic outcomes

Sales less logged post-harvest costs can be compared across recorded batches. This excludes unlogged costs and does not establish total farming profit.

Progress starts with participation

These focus areas describe the platform's purpose. We have not yet published measured outcomes for farmer income, harvest yields or environmental change.

Proposed pilot: agree the protocol with a cooperative and buyer, collect a baseline, record comparable batches, then review results before making impact claims.
